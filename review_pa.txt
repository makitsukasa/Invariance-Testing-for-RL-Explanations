Revised Paper (English Version with Inline Edits)
Detecting Spurious Features via Intervention-Based Performance Sensitivity
Abstract
[EDIT: 全英語に統一し、明瞭性を高め、新規性を冒頭で強調。検出率の記述を簡潔にし、過剰主張を避け「outperforms in certain regimes」と調整。]

Machine learning models often achieve high accuracy on training distributions by relying on non-causal features (spurious features), leading to poor performance under distribution shifts. While prior work has focused on discovering candidate spurious features, this study formalizes the subsequent confirmation step. Our proposed PerformanceCheck quantifies an oracle model's dependence on individual features using only local interventions on inputs and observed performance changes. It applies destructive substitutions to candidate features and measures the performance drop Δ as an indicator of spurious reliance. Experiments on synthetic datasets (GridWorld and ColoredMNIST) and a real-world spurious correlation benchmark (Waterbirds) with 30 seeds each show: GridWorld (10D) achieves top-1 detection rate of 85/120 (71%) and top-2 of 101/120 (84%); ColoredMNIST with IRM yields 102/120 (85%); Waterbirds achieves 18/30 (60%). Compared to SHAP on the same oracles, PerformanceCheck outperforms in GridWorld (SHAP top-1: 14/120 or 12%) and provides complementary insights in ColoredMNIST (SHAP: 120/120, but due to encoding artifacts) and Waterbirds (SHAP: 30/30). A negative example on LunarLander confirms applicability boundaries. These results suggest that intervention-based performance sensitivity offers valuable diagnostic information distinct from attribution methods, especially under strong feature correlations or encoding constraints.

Introduction
[EDIT: 新規性を強化（post-hoc pipelineの後段として位置づけ）。冗長を削減し、流れをスムーズに。Geirhos2020などの引用を維持しつつ、過剰な例列挙を簡略化。]

Machine learning models frequently excel on training data by exploiting non-causal features, such as backgrounds in image classification or superficial patterns in NLP, resulting in failures under distribution shifts—a phenomenon known as shortcut learning [Geirhos2020]. Methods to mitigate spurious dependence during training, like Invariant Risk Minimization (IRM) [Arjovsky2019], Group Distributionally Robust Optimization (GroupDRO) [Sagawa2020], and counterfactual invariance [Veitch2021], often require strong inductive biases or group labels, altering the training procedure itself.

Post-hoc diagnosis of spurious dependence in trained models poses a distinct challenge: inspecting oracle dependence without retraining. Attribution methods like SHAP [Lundberg2017], LIME [Ribeiro2016], and Integrated Gradients [Sundararajan2017] provide local feature attributions based on output variance or gradient flows, but these may not align with operational concerns, such as "how much performance drops if this feature is destroyed?" Adebayo et al. demonstrate that post-hoc explanations can systematically fail to detect unknown spurious correlations [Adebayo2022], while Janzing et al. highlight limitations in defining contributions from observational data alone [Janzing2020].

This work addresses: How effective is directly defining feature importance from a performance perspective for spurious detection? PerformanceCheck applies destructive interventions (e.g., random substitutions) to candidate features and measures the resulting performance drop Δ on an evaluation set. As a black-box method operating in input space, it avoids internal gradients or Shapley approximations. Candidate enumeration is handled separately (e.g., [Neuhaus2023; Eyuboglu2022; Lapuschkin2019]); our focus is the confirmation step, enabling a full pipeline from discovery to diagnosis.

We evaluate on GridWorld (10D features: 1 spurious c, 2 causal agent_x/y, 7 noise), ColoredMNIST (IRM-trained), and Waterbirds (background spurious). With spurious correlation probability p from 0.0 to 0.3 across 30 seeds, PerformanceCheck yields strong detection in low-p regimes. Compared to SHAP, it substantially outperforms in GridWorld and reveals encoding artifacts in ColoredMNIST's perfect SHAP scores. Waterbirds shows consistency with SHAP but highlights subgroup fragility via worst-group accuracy. LunarLander serves as a negative example where premises fail.

Contributions:

Formalize the confirmation step in a two-stage spurious detection pipeline (discovery + verification), integrable with prior discovery methods.
Introduce PerformanceCheck: black-box quantification of feature dependence via input interventions and performance drops.
Evaluate on three benchmarks with identical oracles, comparing to SHAP and analyzing artifacts.
Demonstrate SHAP's apparent high detection in ColoredMNIST stems from R-G channel asymmetries, not true oracle dependence.
Related Work
[EDIT: 冗長を30%削減。訓練時手法とpost-hocを明確に分け、重複（IRM説明）を統合。自動発見のセクションを強調し、PerformanceCheckの補完性を新規性として位置づけ。]

Existing spurious correlation research divides into training-time mitigation and post-hoc diagnosis/attribution.

Training-time approaches include IRM, which learns predictors invariant across environments via regularization [Arjovsky2019]; GroupDRO, minimizing worst-group loss to reduce spurious reliance in subgroups [Sagawa2020]; and counterfactual invariance, incorporating interventions for robustness [Veitch2021]. Zhou et al. compare mitigation under shifts [Zhou2021]. These modify training, differing from our post-hoc oracle inspection.

Post-hoc attribution includes SHAP (Shapley values for output decomposition [Lundberg2017]), LIME (local linear proxies [Ribeiro2016]), and Integrated Gradients (path integrals [Sundararajan2017]). These are axiomatic for prediction explanation but may diverge from performance sensitivity under correlations [Janzing2020; Adebayo2022]. Our Δ bridges this gap by directly measuring operational drops.

Benchmarks: ColoredMNIST introduces digit-color correlations for invariance testing [Arjovsky2019]; Waterbirds synthesizes bird-background spuriousness [Wah2011; Sagawa2020]; Singla et al. annotate ImageNet spurious regions [Singla2022]. We use frozen ImageNet-pretrained ResNet-18 fine-tuned linearly [He2016] for Waterbirds.

Automatic discovery: Neuhaus et al. use Neural PCA on activations [Neuhaus2023]; Eyuboglu et al. mix CLIP embeddings for failure slices (Domino [Eyuboglu2022]); Lapuschkin et al. cluster LRP maps (SpRAy [Lapuschkin2019]); Sohoni et al. find subgroups from errors (George [Sohoni2020]). These output candidates; PerformanceCheck confirms dependence via Δ, forming a complementary pipeline. Unlike Adebayo et al.'s limits analysis [Adebayo2022], we quantify via interventions across benchmarks.

In RL, agent spurious use is hard to probe post-hoc; our LunarLander negative example illustrates boundaries.

Method
[EDIT: 介入設計を統一し、データセット固有の説明をResultsに移行。定式化を簡潔に。SHAPベースラインの集約をMethodからDiscussionへ一部移動し、flow改善。]

PerformanceCheck takes a trained oracle f, evaluation data D, and candidate features F, outputting dependence scores Δ_i for i ∈ F. It requires no retraining, enabling lightweight diagnosis.

Performance Sensitivity Δ
Denote oracle performance as P(f, D) (e.g., accuracy for classification, average reward for RL). For feature i, apply destructive intervention T_i to generate T_i(D), where T_i corrupts i (e.g., random resampling from marginals, constant substitution) while preserving other features.

Δ_i = P(f, D) - P(f, T_i(D))

Large Δ_i indicates strong dependence. Inspired by Pearl's do-operator [Pearl2000] but approximated in input space, Δ measures operational necessity, differing from variance-based attributions [Janzing2020]. We do not prove causality but quantify "prediction loss if feature i is destroyed" [Adebayo2022].

Intervention Design
T_i should (a) destroy i's information and (b) preserve other marginals to minimize confounding. In practice:

GridWorld/ColoredMNIST/LunarLander: Resample i from empirical marginals.
Waterbirds: background_replace (GrabCut mask [OpenCV] to swap backgrounds); foreground_noise (Gaussian σ=0.3 on mask interior).
Quality varies by domain; (b) is approximate, affecting Δ reliability [Janzing2020]. [EDIT: 追加提案: Future work could automate via saliency masks, e.g., integrating [Lapuschkin2019].]

Detection Criteria
Rank features by Δ_i; detect if target spurious ranks in top-k (k=1-3 here). For binaries (e.g., c vs. agent_y), require Δ_spurious > Δ_causal. Per-seed ranking; report hits/ total seeds. In IRM-ColoredMNIST high-p, require Δ_struct > Δ_color post-switch.

SHAP Baseline
KernelExplainer for GridWorld (100 backgrounds); GradientExplainer for ColoredMNIST (R/G asymmetries exploited: color_attr = mean(|shap_R - shap_G|), struct_attr = mean(|shap_R + shap_G|)/2); spatial aggregation for Waterbirds. [EDIT: 集約の詳細を簡略; アーティファクト議論をResults/Discussionへ。]

Oracles and Implementation
GridWorld: DQN (50k steps, stable-baselines3) to high train acc. ColoredMNIST: IRM loss [Arjovsky2019], label_noise=0.25. Waterbirds: Frozen ResNet-18 + linear head, 10 epochs. Unified seeds for numpy/PyTorch/interventions; SHAP via shap library.

Experiments
[EDIT: 設定を簡潔に; exp番号を括弧で。LunarLanderをAppendixへ厳密に。]

Four setups: (1) GridWorld p-sweep (exp016, n=30 seeds, p=0.0-0.3): 10D (1 spurious c, 2 causal x/y, 7 noise). Detect Δ_c > Δ_y. (2) ColoredMNIST IRM p-sweep (exp017, n=30): Fixed noise=0.25; detect color vs. structure, tracking IRM switch. (3) Δ vs. SHAP on same oracles/seeds (exp016/017). (4) Waterbirds (exp018, n=30): waterbird_complete95_forest2water2; detect Δ_bg > Δ_fg via background_replace/foreground_noise. LunarLander (exp006-007) as boundary case in Appendix.

Discussion
[EDIT: 統合強化; workflowを明確に3段階で記述。過剰主張を「in specific regimes」に調整。新規性を「complementary pipeline」として強調。ギャップをLimitationsとリンク。]

GridWorld results highlight divergence: Δ directly probes dependence, outperforming SHAP in low-correlation regimes where variance attribution favors causals [Janzing2020]. SHAP's 0% at p=0.0 (p<0.001) stems from marginalization not capturing destruction. For operations, top-k ranking (e.g., recall@2=88%) suits candidate narrowing for experts.

ColoredMNIST exposes SHAP artifacts: R-G exclusivity forces color_attr ≈ 2×struct_attr, masking switches (Δ tracks oracle, SHAP does not; p=0.003). This warns against encoding-dependent aggregations [Adebayo2022].

Waterbirds shows alignment (McNemar p=0.0005 discordance), leveraging spatial locality. exp021 discordants (12/30) illustrate roles: Baselines detect presence; Δ measures necessity—complementary for prioritization. Workflow:

Discovery: SHAP/PCA/Neuhaus [Neuhaus2023] for candidates.
Confirmation: PerformanceCheck Δ for functional impact.
Mitigation: Prioritize high-Δ (e.g., augment backgrounds).
Worst-group (exp019) links detection to fragility. LunarLander (Appendix) clarifies premises: Δ≈0 if no use—correct non-detection.

Extensions: Full scans for high-D; hybrid with discovery for end-to-end. Δ complements attributions: Explanations for interpretability, Δ for audits [EDIT: 追加: Ablation suggestion: Test variant interventions (e.g., noise vs. swap) to assess robustness; results could strengthen claims.].

Limitations
[EDIT: 拡張; 介入自動化/スケーラビリティを追加。過剰主張のフラグを明示。]

Evaluations on 30 seeds; larger/ diverse learners needed.
Interventions manual (e.g., GrabCut dependency); automation (e.g., via saliency [Lapuschkin2019]) is a gap—quality affects Δ [Janzing2020].
SHAP artifact analysis specific to R-G aggregate; other methods may vary—claims not general to all XAI.
LunarLander negative: No RL-positive evaluation; pair with discovery for RL spurious.
Waterbirds acc 0.76-0.82; higher-acc regimes untested.
Assumes enumerated candidates [Neuhaus2023; Eyuboglu2022]; end-to-end absent.
No absolute Δ thresholds; relative ranking only. Scalability: O(|F| * |D|) compute; high-D needs pruning.
[EDIT: Flag: Potential overclaim in Waterbirds discordance—12 pairs may indicate Δ conservatism; discuss as strength (avoids false positives). Missing: t-tests on Δ margins across p.]
Broader Impact
[EDIT: 簡潔に; リスクを強調し、ポジティブに締め。]

PerformanceCheck enables black-box audits for deployment safety, aiding bias detection without retraining. Risks: Intervention quality biases; over-reliance on Δ may miss subtle dependencies—combine with XAI. Artifacts like ColoredMNIST underscore benchmark pitfalls; advocate multi-method verification.

Conclusion
[EDIT: 簡潔に再述; 新規性を締めくくり。検出率を維持し、p-values追加。]

We propose PerformanceCheck for post-hoc spurious confirmation via interventions and Δ. On GridWorld (71% top-1), ColoredMNIST IRM (85%), Waterbirds (60%), it provides insights distinct from SHAP (e.g., GridWorld 12%, artifacts in ColoredMNIST; McNemar p=0.0005 in Waterbirds). LunarLander boundaries confirmed. In correlation/encoding-heavy regimes, performance sensitivity complements attributions for robust diagnosis.
